# ENT101 – Porter Case Study

This group project involved an in-depth study of **Porter**, an Indian logistics startup, focusing on its early-stage journey. After more than two weeks of research and analysis, the team submitted a 9-page presentation along with a 3-minute pitch summarizing our findings.
